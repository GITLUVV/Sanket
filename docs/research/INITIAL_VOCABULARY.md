# Initial ISL Constrained Vocabulary Definition — Sanket

## 1. Principles of Vocabulary Selection
To ensure manageable engineering complexity and high scientific validity during Phase 1, the initial vocabulary must fulfill four criteria:
1. **Utility**: High-frequency communication terms (greetings, emergency, basic needs).
2. **Morphological Balance**: A mix of one-handed and two-handed signs with distinguishable hand configurations.
3. **Static vs Movement Balance**: Signs with identifiable key static handshapes suitable for initial baseline testing before temporal modeling.
4. **Signer Safety & Accessibility**: Respect canonical Indian Sign Language grammar and conventions.

## 2. Proposed Phase 1 Constrained Vocabulary (15 Signs)

| Index | Gloss / Label | Category | Handedness | Dominant Movement | Linguistic Notes |
| :---- | :------------ | :------- | :--------- | :---------------- | :--------------- |
| 1 | `HELLO` / `NAMASTE` | Greeting | Two-Handed | Static / Contact | Palms together near chest (Anjali mudra) |
| 2 | `THANK_YOU` | Courtesy | One-Handed | Forward motion | Flat hand touches chin and extends forward |
| 3 | `YES` | Affirmation | One-Handed | Vertical tilt | Fist nods downward like a head nodding |
| 4 | `NO` | Negation | One-Handed | Lateral shake | Index finger extended or index+middle shaking |
| 5 | `PLEASE` | Courtesy | One-Handed | Circular | Flat open palm rubs circular motion on chest |
| 6 | `HELP` | Emergency | Two-Handed | Upward lift | Closed fist placed on open supporting palm, lifting |
| 7 | `WATER` | Daily Need | One-Handed | Contact to mouth | 'W' handshape or cupped hand towards mouth |
| 8 | `FOOD` / `EAT` | Daily Need | One-Handed | Contact to mouth | Flattened O-hand tips toward mouth |
| 9 | `DOCTOR` | Healthcare | Two-Handed | Tap wrist | Fingers tap wrist (feeling pulse metaphor) |
| 10 | `STOP` | Directive | Two-Handed | Contact | Dominant knife-hand strikes flat non-dominant palm |
| 11 | `NAME` | Identity | Two-Handed | Contact | Extended index and middle fingers cross/tap |
| 12 | `WHERE` | Query | Two-Handed | Upward oscillation | Palms facing upward oscillating side-to-side |
| 13 | `TIME` | Query | One-Handed | Tap wrist | Index finger taps back of non-dominant wrist |
| 14 | `GOOD` | Evaluation | One-Handed | Static / Upward | Thumbs-up posture |
| 15 | `BAD` | Evaluation | One-Handed | Downward | Thumbs-down or hand flick |
