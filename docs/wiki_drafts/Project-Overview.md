# Project Overview

## Mission & Vision
Indian Sign Language (ISL) is used by millions of Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing individuals across India. Despite its prevalence, digital accessibility tools tailored to ISL syntax, phonology, and regional variations remain severely limited compared to American Sign Language (ASL) or British Sign Language (BSL).

**Sanket (संकेत)** bridges this communication gap by creating a robust, privacy-respecting, real-time AI accessibility interface.

## Scope & Non-Overclaiming Clause
Sanket enforces strict empirical development gates:
- **Phase 1** focuses strictly on a **constrained vocabulary of 16 isolated signs** to establish reproducible baseline performance.
- We do not claim continuous, open-vocabulary sentence translation during early phases.
- Real-time webcam integration (Phase 2), dynamic temporal modeling (Phase 3), and grammar/speech translation (Phase 4) follow sequentially upon empirical validation.
