# Indian Sign Language (ISL) Linguistic Background

Indian Sign Language (ISL) is a complete, natural human language with its own autonomous grammar, phonology, and syntactic structure.

## The Five Parameters of ISL Phonology
In sign language linguistics, signs are formed by five core parameters (cheremes):
1. **Handshape**: The configuration of the fingers and thumb (e.g., flat palm, fist, index point).
2. **Location / Place of Articulation**: Where the sign is made relative to the signer's body (neutral space, chest, chin, forehead).
3. **Movement**: The trajectory, repetition, or rotation of the hand through space.
4. **Palm Orientation**: The direction the palm faces (upward, downward, toward the signer, toward the camera).
5. **Non-Manual Markers (NMM)**: Facial expressions, head tilts, and mouth shapes that convey grammatical mood or negation.
