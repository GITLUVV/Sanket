# ISL Constrained Vocabulary (Phase 1)

Phase 1 benchmarks a curated 16-sign vocabulary selected from the INCLUDE corpus based on communicative utility and phonological diversity:

| Index | Gloss | Category | Articulation | Handedness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | `HELLO` | Greeting | Open palm wave near head | Single-handed |
| 1 | `THANK_YOU` | Etiquette | Flat hand moves forward from chin | Single-handed |
| 2 | `YES` | Affirmation | Fist nods up and down | Single-handed |
| 3 | `NO` | Negation | Index & middle fingers snap to thumb | Single-handed |
| 4 | `PLEASE` | Etiquette | Circular motion on chest | Single-handed |
| 5 | `WATER` | Daily Life | 'W' handshape touches chin | Single-handed |
| 6 | `FOOD` | Daily Life | O-handshape moves toward mouth | Single-handed |
| 7 | `HELP` | Assistance | Thumbs-up resting on flat open palm | Two-handed |
| 8 | `GOOD` | Evaluation | Thumbs-up moves upward | Single-handed |
| 9 | `BAD` | Evaluation | Flat hand turns palm down abruptly | Single-handed |
| 10 | `FAMILY` | Social | Both 'F' hands circle together | Two-handed |
| 11 | `FRIEND` | Social | Interlocking index fingers | Two-handed |
| 12 | `WORK` | Activity | One fist taps wrist of other fist | Two-handed |
| 13 | `NAME` | Identity | 'H' fingers tap crosswise | Two-handed |
| 14 | `WHERE` | Question | Both open hands move side to side | Two-handed |
| 15 | `HOW` | Question | Knuckles together, palms rotate up | Two-handed |
