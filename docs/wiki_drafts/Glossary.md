# Glossary

- **ISL**: Indian Sign Language.
- **ISLR**: Indian Sign Language Recognition.
- **Gloss**: An English word capitalized representing a specific sign (e.g. `HELLO`, `THANK_YOU`).
- **Chereme / Phoneme**: The fundamental structural building blocks of signs (handshape, location, movement, palm orientation, non-manual markers).
- **Landmark**: A 3D spatial coordinate $(x, y, z)$ representing an anatomical joint or keypoint.
- **Signer Independence**: Evaluation protocol ensuring signers in the test split never appear during training.
- **Signer Leakage**: A data leakage defect where a model memorizes individual signer characteristics rather than sign kinematics.
- **MCC**: Matthews Correlation Coefficient, measuring multi-class classification quality.
- **ADR**: Architecture Decision Record, documenting significant design choices.
